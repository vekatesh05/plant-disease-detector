Which plants and conditions are supported?

The model includes selected categories for apple, blueberry, cherry, corn, grape, orange, peach, bell pepper, potato, raspberry, soybean, squash, strawberry and tomato. Coverage differs by crop; not every disease is included.

The classifier always selects from its known plant-disease categories, even when the uploaded photo is unrelated or shows an unknown condition. Predictions for non-plant photos are not meaningful diagnoses. A "healthy" result does not rule out other plant problems.

Educational screening tool. Confirm suspected disease with a plant specialist before choosing treatment.
